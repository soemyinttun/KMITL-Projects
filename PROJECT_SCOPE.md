# Project Scope: SSM Travel & Tours Co., Ltd. App

## What is this?
- One app for your travel company that works on a phone and on a computer.
- It works even when there is no internet.
- It helps you keep track of customers, trips, visas, passports and flight tickets.
- It shows which services make the most money and where your customers want to go.

## What do we want to achieve?
- Staff can work all day, even without internet.
- No more notes on paper or in many different files. Everything is in one place.
- The owner can quickly see what makes the most money.
- The owner can see on a map which places are most popular.
- The app warns you about late visa cases, passports that will expire soon, and trips that are not ready.

## Services in the app
- **Travel packages:** India and Bhutan, with 4, 7, 8, 12 and 15-day trips.
- **Visa service:** India and Bhutan visas.
- **Passport service:** new, renewal, lost and damaged passports.
- **Air tickets:** flights coming into Myanmar and flights leaving Myanmar.

## What is included (Version 1)

### Money and comparison screens
- **Home screen:** total money earned, profit, number of sales, and a chart for each month.
- **Money comparison:**
  - See money earned by service, country, trip length, destination or customer town.
  - Compare any two things side by side, for example India vs Bhutan.
  - Tap a destination to see only that place everywhere in the app.
- **Package comparison:**
  - India vs Bhutan for all trip lengths.
  - Shows price, cost, profit, how many people asked, and how many booked.
  - You can change prices yourself.
- **Heat maps:**
  - A map that glows brighter for the most popular places.
  - One map shows where travellers want to go. Another shows which Myanmar towns they come from.
  - You can switch between people who asked, people who booked, and money earned.

### Daily work screens
- **Visa:** move each case step by step: Enquiry, Documents, Submitted, Approved or Rejected, Delivered. Shows deadlines and how often visas are approved.
- **Passport:** move each case step by step: Enquiry, Documents, Submitted, Ready, Collected.
- **Air tickets:** add flights, see the best routes and airlines, and see your commission.
- **Bookings:** add a new booking, confirm it, finish it or cancel it. You can search for any booking.
- **Customers:** a list of customers with phone numbers and passport dates. Passports that will expire in under 6 months are shown in colour.

### Calendar
- A calendar showing trips, visa deadlines, passport deadlines and flights.
- A button to add any event to your Google Calendar.
- A button to download the calendar so you can open it in other calendar apps.
- A "ready to travel?" list for the next 45 days. It checks passport, visa and flight for each traveller.

### Other things
- Install it on the phone's home screen like a normal app.
- Works without internet. Information is saved on your own phone or computer.
- Filters at the top: time period, service, place, and money type (US dollars or Myanmar kyat).
- Save a backup copy of your information, and bring it back when needed.
- Download your sales list as a spreadsheet file.
- Sample (fake) information is included so you can try every screen straight away.

## What is NOT included (Version 1)
- Taking payments online.
- Live flight prices or live booking with airlines.
- A website or app for customers to book by themselves.
- Sending visa or passport forms to the government automatically.
- Reminder messages by phone, SMS or email.
- Different logins for different staff.
- Sharing information between two phones (each phone keeps its own information).
- Burmese language (English only for now).
- Invoices, tax and salary.

## What we are assuming
- One company, one office, a small team (about 1 to 10 people).
- All money is saved in US dollars. Kyat is worked out using a rate you type in.
- The prices in the app now are only examples. You need to type in your real prices.
- For Bhutan, your price should include the government tourism fee. You type in the current amount.
- Each staff member uses their own phone or computer, and the information is not shared yet.

## How do we know it is a success?
- A staff member can add a booking, move a visa case, and look at the screens with the phone in airplane mode.
- The app can be installed on Android and iPhone and opens full screen.
- The owner can see the most profitable service, package and place in about two taps.
- The money numbers on screen match the real records.

## Things to be careful about
- **Information stays on one phone:** if a phone is lost or wiped, the information is gone unless you saved a backup. Please make backups often. Sharing between phones will fix this later.
- **iPhone:** iPhone may delete saved information if the app is not used for a long time. Install it on the home screen to be safe.
- **Map background:** the first time you look at a new area, you need internet to see the map picture. The glowing spots still show without internet.
- **Example prices:** the numbers are only as good as the prices and costs you type in.
